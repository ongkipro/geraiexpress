## Summary

At least 14 aggregator-style programs and 9 official courier agent programs recruit Indonesian counters. Aggregator programs (AutoKirim, Fastpay, SIMASRIM, bosAgen, UKURIR, Everpro, Kurir Pedia, Lincah, Komship, KiriminAja, Mitra Bukalapak) are mostly free to join. They advertise 20–40% "komisi/cashback/diskon", courier pickup, COD and thermal labels, and earn money from optional paid equipment bundles. Official courier programs (JNE, Lion Parcel, SAPX, J&T MDP, Agenpos, SiCepat S&DP, Mitra Ninja) are single-brand. They require more paperwork (KTP, NPWP, location photos, often NIB/KBLI and SKCK/domicile letters), distance rules of 500 m–1 km, equipment and sometimes deposits or fees of Rp350k–Rp10M, plus sales targets (JNE). Customer-facing WhatsApp notification and a printed counter receipt are advertised by only a few vendors: UKURIR (WhatsApp), SIMASRIM (receipt from its EDC device) and Kurir Pedia (WhatsApp/email). No counter vendor read advertises that a walk-in counter's identity can replace the sender on the label. The nearest offers are UKURIR's paid "Mitra Premium" white-label resi (resi carrying the partner's own brand) and dropship-sender features in seller tools (OnOrder; Mengantar and KiriminAja per the earlier note).

## Findings

1. **Claim:** Aggregator agent programs are free to *register*, but the larger ones sell optional starter bundles, so "gratis" is not the whole cost story. AutoKirim's agent page lists a Rp0 tier plus "Personal" Rp1,999,999 and "Bisnis" Rp4,999,999 bundles (scale, scanner, 80/100 mm printer, banner, balance bonus). An older AutoKirim landing page also lists "Prioritas" at Rp25.9 jt. Fastpay sells expedition packages at Rp49k, Rp299k (with scale) and Rp899k (with scale and printer). Separately, its "Jutawan" PPOB package needs a Rp1.1 jt first deposit, and the Rp100k deposit-box portion is forfeited unless the agent makes 300 transactions in 30 days. **Sources:** [AutoKirim agen](https://autokirim.com/layanan/agen), [AutoKirim /agen](https://autokirim.com/agen), [fastxpress.id](https://fastxpress.id/), [daftar.geraifastpay.co.id](https://daftar.geraifastpay.co.id/agen/fastxpress). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high (that the vendors say this).

2. **Claim:** Advertised earning rates cluster at 20–40% for aggregators and are inconsistent *within* the same vendor. AutoKirim says "Cashback hingga 40%" on its homepage and agent page, "hingga 30%" in its homepage FAQ, "Komisi mulai dari 25%" on /agen, and "hingga 25%" in its App Store listing. SIMASRIM's homepage says "s.d 25%", while its instant-resi page lists SAPX REG 20%, J&T EZ 10%, SPX 20%, J&T Cargo 5% and Lion REGPACK 10%. Komship says "hingga 50%" in its hero and 25–40% in its per-courier table. Lincah shows a per-courier table (SAPX 30%, J&T 35% COD/30% non-COD, JNE 25%/20%, Ninja 50%/40% marked "Tidak Aktif"). **Sources:** [AutoKirim](https://autokirim.com/), [SIMASRIM cetak resi](https://app.simasrim.com/cetak_resi_instan/buat), [Komship](https://komship.id/), [Lincah](https://lincah.id/). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high that the figures are published, low that any is a typical realized rate. Inference: headline rates are "up to" maxima for specific courier/service mixes.

3. **Claim:** Official courier programs publish rates comparable to or above aggregator headlines, which contradicts Fastpay's blog framing that courier agents earn "Rp1.000–3.000 per paket". Lion Parcel's appointment terms (PDF, labelled 1 Feb 2026) state 35% Bosspack, 30% Regpack, 25% Jagopack/Bigpack, 20% Interpack, and a flat Rp4,000/kg (pickup) or Rp1,000/kg (drop) for marketplace parcels. Those terms also cut the discount by 10% if the agent uses Lion pickup. SAPX advertises 25% plus a 1% COD commission. Fastpay's own blog labels its courier-commission figures "rata-rata lapangan, bukan klaim resmi". **Sources:** [Lion Parcel S&K PDF](https://storage.googleapis.com/internal-website/agent/syarat-ketentuan-agen-lion-parcel.pdf), [SAPX kemitraan](https://www.sapx.id/id/layanan/kemitraan), [Fastpay blog](https://www.fastpay.co.id/blog/berapa-komisi-agen-ekspedisi.html). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high.

4. **Claim:** Official programs carry much heavier entry requirements than aggregators. JNE: SCRF form, KTP, bank statement for the last 3 months, sales-equipment fee and cash deposit set per branch, proof of location ownership/lease, SKDU, HO permit, PBB receipt. JNE also requires a ruko/kiosk of at least 12 m² with AC and a ceramic floor, 4-wheel access, at least 1,300 W power, 2 PCs, a laser printer, and a 30 kg table scale plus a 100 kg floor scale re-calibrated (tera) at least yearly. Further JNE conditions: at least 1 km from another JNE counter, a 3-month trial sales target of Rp2 jt–Rp50 jt by branch grade, daily deposit of sales net of commission by H+1, and at most 2 counters per owner. Lion Parcel's web page says "no royalty… just KTP and location photos", but its PDF terms require KTP, NPWP, KK, bank book, an admin staff member, a PC/laptop, CCTV, 500 m spacing, and a Rp10,000,000 administration payment (of which Rp500k is a non-refundable deposit and Rp1M operating balance; the kit includes a 60 kg scale and a thermal printer). SAPX: KTP and NPWP, 1 admin, room of at least 2×3 m, 4-wheel access, computer, resi printer, internet, at least 500 m from another SAPX agent, a site survey, and a fee from Rp350k (kit includes a 100 kg scale and a Rp50k–500k deposit). **Sources:** [JNE keagenan](https://jne.co.id/agent), [Lion Parcel agen](https://lionparcel.com/agen), [Lion Parcel PDF](https://storage.googleapis.com/internal-website/agent/syarat-ketentuan-agen-lion-parcel.pdf), [SAPX kemitraan](https://www.sapx.id/id/layanan/kemitraan). **Support:** direct evidence (T1 for the courier's own program). **Confidence:** high.

5. **Claim:** J&T Express's "Mini Drop Point" (MDP, April 2025) recruits individuals or companies through branches. Individuals need a KTP, NPWP and active bank account. Companies need deed, NIB with KBLI 53202, company NPWP, PKP/non-PKP letter and bank account. Both must submit location coordinates and front/inside photos. Benefits are commission, training, branding and a reward program for high-volume partners. No rate or fee is published. **Sources:** [businessreview.id reproducing J&T press statement](https://businessreview.id/2025/04/22/jt-express-ajak-masyarakat-gabung-mitra-mini-drop-point-raih-komisi-dengan-modal-kemitraan-terjangkau/). **Support:** T3 reproduction of an official press release; jet.co.id itself was not read. **Confidence:** medium.

6. **Claim:** UKURIR is the only counter vendor read that advertises **customer WhatsApp notification** *and* a **white-label tier with a branded resi**. Its "Mitra Drop Point" tier is free, with no deposit and no target, and lists "WhatsApp notification kepada pelanggan". Drop-point requirements are KTP, KK, photo, domicile letter from the kelurahan, a recent SKCK, location plan, 4-wheel access, and the partner's own scale, label printer, measuring tool and computer/tablet. "Mitra Premium (White Label)" costs Rp300k/month (Rp2.5 jt/year). It offers "White label resi menggunakan brand Anda", "WhatsApp notification menggunakan brand Anda", own markup and own first-mile fee, and requires a business entity, own domain, NIB and NPWP. **Sources:** [UKURIR kemitraan](https://ukurir.com/id-id/mitra). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high that UKURIR says so. Unknown: what the white-label resi actually prints, whether the courier's own label still carries UKURIR or courier identity, and whether customers receive notifications in practice.

7. **Claim:** Receipt/kasir and QRIS appear as a hardware upsell, not as a counter-software feature. SIMASRIM sells Android EDC terminals with thermal printers that "input paket, cetak resi… cetak struk" (KYC with KTP, KK and a business-location photo; Mini-ATM units need a KB Bank escrow account). It also sells a separate "SQRIS Soundbox" that announces received QRIS payments. No other counter program read advertises in-app QRIS collection or a customer receipt. JNE's official kit includes a paper "Bukti Tanda Terima Pembayaran (Kwitansi)" book. KirimAja users pay in-app by bank transfer, credit card, DOKU, OVO or LinkAja (QRIS not mentioned). **Sources:** [SIMASRIM](https://www.simasrim.com/), [SIMASRIM EDC](https://simasrim.com/edc), [JNE keagenan](https://jne.co.id/agent), [KirimAja FAQ](https://www.kirimaja.id/faq). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** medium (absence on marketing pages is not proof of absence in the apps).

8. **Claim:** Claims/returns handling is stated by a few vendors, with different promises. AutoKirim's FAQ: insured shipments are replaced at the declared value, uninsured at 10× the shipping fee, handled through its CS WhatsApp; its agent page says "Gratis Biaya Ongkir Paket Retur". SIMASRIM shows per-courier T&C at order time (unboxing video required; loss claims within 30 calendar days of last status). Kurir Pedia advertises "Garansi 100%… ganti senilai barang + ongkir… cair maksimal 3×24 jam". KirimAja routes refunds and claims through a WhatsApp form. Komship advertises "gratis retur se-Indonesia" and RTS-rate analytics. **Sources:** [AutoKirim](https://autokirim.com/), [AutoKirim /agen](https://autokirim.com/agen), [SIMASRIM cetak resi](https://app.simasrim.com/cetak_resi_instan/buat), [Kurir Pedia](https://kurirpedia.com/), [KirimAja FAQ](https://www.kirimaja.id/faq), [Komship](https://komship.id/). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** medium. Inference: Kurir Pedia's 100% guarantee exceeds the 10× rule other vendors cite. Treat it as an unverified marketing promise.

9. **Claim:** Sender masking / dropship sender name **for counters** is not advertised by any counter vendor read. Seller-side tools do offer it: OnOrder's storefront dropship sends "atas nama reseller (nama dan nomor telepon dropshipper)", and Komship's API order body has `shipper_name`/`shipper_phone` fields (snippet only). Mengantar and KiriminAja were covered in the earlier note (S2, S26). UKURIR's white-label resi (finding 6) is the closest counter-facing offer, but it brands the platform operator, not each walk-in sender. **Sources:** [OnOrder dropship help](https://help.onorder.id/order-dropship), [Komship API (snippet)](https://developer.komship.id/reference/api-reference/order), [UKURIR](https://ukurir.com/id-id/mitra). **Support:** direct evidence for OnOrder/UKURIR; snippet for Komship. **Confidence:** medium. Kurir Pedia's FAQ goes the other way: it requires full sender name, address and phone on the label per the courier's standard.

10. **Claim:** Several aggregators resell through other aggregators, so "official partner" claims are layered. SIMASRIM's guide says JNE is now processed "melalui partner (aggregator)". AutoKirim claims agents become "drop point resmi ekspedisi". Fastpay (2021 blog) claims to be SiCepat's official partner for SiCepat Point registration. **Sources:** [SIMASRIM docs (snippet)](https://docs.simasrim.com/53b8f03d-66ee-46a8-94fc-ac7338e03948/docs-page.php?section=pengiriman-jne), [AutoKirim /agen](https://autokirim.com/agen), [Fastpay SiCepat Point blog (snippet, 2021, stale)](https://www.fastpay.co.id/blog/daftar-menjadi-agen-sicepat-point-resmi-di-fastpay.html). **Support:** snippet/direct mix. **Confidence:** low–medium. Inference: GeraiExpress-on-Mengantar would sit in the same layered position, so courier-side rules can change under it.

11. **Claim:** KirimAja (Garuda group) is a hybrid. "Sohib" users who drop at a Drop Point or SubConsole receive up to 50%/40% (≤10 kg) or 25%/15% (>10 kg) as an upfront fee cut, and nothing for pickup. SubConsoles verify item type and weight before the sender pays, may refuse parcels, earn 10% of the shipping fee paid by bank transfer twice a month, carry the parcel to a KirimAja Drop Point themselves, and are responsible for it until handover. **Sources:** [KirimAja FAQ](https://www.kirimaja.id/faq). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high.

12. **Claim:** Paxel's agent options are either a company-only "PaxelCo" regional partnership (PT/CV required) or "Paxel Agen Tanpa Modal" (online, 9% per shipment, selection by Paxel; article dated 2023-03-13, possibly stale). **Sources:** [Paxel mitra](https://paxel.co/id/mitrapaxel), [Paxel ATM article](https://paxel.co/id/berita-dan-promo/mau-hasilkan-uang-dari-internet-dengan-mudah-gabung-jadi-paxel-agen-tanpa-modal). **Support:** direct evidence; the form requirement is a snippet. **Confidence:** medium.

13. **Claim:** Anteraja's and ID Express's official partner pages target sellers and corporates (Anteraja PRO with free insurance below Rp1 jt; iDexpress VIP Seller with free pickup and COD payout in 1 working day), not counter agents. ID Express drop-off runs through its own outlets and Alfamart/Alfamidi/Dan+Dan. No open counter-agent program was found on either official site. **Sources:** [Anteraja partner (snippet; page rendered empty)](https://anteraja.id/partner-with-us/home), [iDexpress berpartner](https://idexpress.com/berpartner-dengan-kami), [iDexpress drop-off (snippet)](https://idexpress.com/bantuan/lokasi-drop-off-paket). **Support:** direct for iDexpress, snippet for Anteraja. **Confidence:** medium.

## Derived synthesis

**Table stakes** (advertised by at least 5 of the counter-oriented vendors read): multi-courier quote and booking in one account; free registration with no subscription; a percentage "komisi/cashback/diskon" headline (20–40%); courier pickup at the counter; COD support; balance/saldo top-up model; resi tracking inside the dashboard or app; CS via WhatsApp; Android app or mobile-capable web.

**Common but not universal** (2–4 vendors): PPOB/bill payments (Fastpay, SIMASRIM, bosAgen, Mitra Bukalapak); paid starter kits with scale and thermal printer (AutoKirim, Fastpay, SAPX, Lion); RTS/return-fee waivers (AutoKirim, Komship, Lincah per courier); agent statistics (bosAgen, Komship analytics); API/plugins (Kurir Pedia, SIMASRIM, UKURIR, AutoKirim, Komship).

**Rare** (at most 1–2 vendors): customer WhatsApp notification (UKURIR; Kurir Pedia claims WA/email); printed customer receipt or EDC/QRIS at the counter (SIMASRIM hardware only); white-label brand (UKURIR Mitra Premium); multi-branch HQ dashboard (SIMASRIM); weight/type verification step before payment (KirimAja SubConsole); configurable split with sub-agents (AutoKirim); credit/TOP with auto-lock (UKURIR corporate). Not seen at all on counter pages: staff roles with audit trail, shift/day cash close, explicit duplicate-resi protection.

**Sender masking / dropship name on label for counters:** none of the counter vendors read advertises it. Closest: UKURIR "white label resi menggunakan brand Anda" (paid, entity-only); OnOrder dropship sender name/phone (seller OMS); Komship API `shipper_name` field (snippet). Inference: GeraiExpress's masked-label concept is still not a visible competitor claim. As the earlier note says, it is unproven until a courier scans the label. Kurir Pedia's FAQ explicitly requires a full and correct sender name, address and phone per the courier's standard, which is a caution for masking.

**Typical agent requirements** (synthesis of official and aggregator programs):
- Identity: KTP (all); NPWP (JNE, J&T, Lion, SAPX, Agenpos); KK (Lion individual, UKURIR, SIMASRIM EDC); SKCK and domicile letter (UKURIR); NIB with KBLI 53202 or courier-agent KBLI for companies (J&T, Agenpos).
- Location: front/inside photos and map coordinates (Lion, J&T, SIMASRIM EDC); 4-wheel access (JNE, SAPX, UKURIR); minimum spacing of 500 m (Lion, SAPX) or 1 km (JNE); minimum size of 2×3 m (SAPX) or 12 m² (JNE).
- Equipment: scale (JNE 30 kg table and 100 kg floor with yearly tera; SAPX 100 kg; Lion 60 kg; AutoKirim 40 kg); label/thermal printer (UKURIR, SAPX, Lion kit, AutoKirim kit) or laser printer (JNE); measuring tape (UKURIR, SAPX, Lion); PC/laptop/tablet plus internet; CCTV (Lion).
- Staff and money: 1 admin (Lion, SAPX); training (JNE, Lion, SAPX); deposits of Rp50k–Rp500k (SAPX) or larger and set per branch (JNE); Rp10M fee (Lion); sales target (JNE trial).
- Aggregators mostly ask only for account data and phone verification. The equipment is "you prepare it yourself" (AutoKirim FAQ) or sold as a bundle.

**Implications for GeraiExpress (inference, not evidence):**
1. GeraiExpress's 20% gerai share sits at or below every published aggregator and official rate (Lion 20–35%, SAPX 25%+1% COD). This matches the earlier note's economics concern.
2. The rare features — WhatsApp share-resi, receipt/payment record, branded label — line up with the proposed PR-38/PR-40. They are differentiation candidates, not table stakes. UKURIR is the closest direct competitor on that axis.
3. Official programs' requirements (calibrated scale, receipts, content check forms such as JNE's BPIK and "Pernyataan Isi Kiriman", insurance-refusal form) show what counter operators already expect at intake. They support PR-41/42/43.

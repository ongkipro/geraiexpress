# Lane E — Voice of the counter operator and the walk-in sender (Indonesia)

- Researched: 2026-11 (sources accessed during this run; publication dates are shown per source).
- Scope: what operators of shipping counters (agen, drop point, gerai) and the people sending parcels actually do and complain about, mapped to the counter jobs J1–J15 in `docs/research/geraiexpress-indonesia-market-gap-analysis.md` §4. Read-only research. No repository file was edited.
- Tiers: **T1** = official, regulator, or a primary survey publisher/statistics agency; **T2** = a vendor or platform describing itself, or a reputable outlet relaying a named survey; **T3** = individual first-hand account (owner interview, reader letter, forum post) or a small academic study. **Anecdote** = one person's account. It shows that a pain exists, not how often it happens.
- Labels: **Direct** = stated in the source. **Interp.** = my reading of the source. **Inference** = my own reasoning, not stated in any source.

## Summary

Practitioner evidence for **walk-in counter operators** specifically is thin and mostly anecdotal. Evidence is strong for four things:

1. **Weight and volumetric disputes.** Many first-hand complaints, and an agent describes being caught in the middle.
2. **Lost parcels and slow claims.** Many first-hand complaints, plus one academic survey.
3. **COD and RTS losses.** Strong survey evidence that COD dominates online buying, and an industry-cited 20–30% RTS rate on aggregators.
4. **Shrinking walk-in volume and margin.** Caused by marketplace cashless payment, free pickup and aggregators. Owner interviews and a JNE branch's network data show it.

Evidence for what happens at the cash desk is weak: payment mix at the counter, cash reconciliation, receipts, and "where is my parcel" WhatsApp load. Only owner interviews or a pilot log can close those gaps.

## 1. Findings by theme

### A. Counter economics and volume (J13, J15; context for the whole product)

**E1. Volume and omzet fell after the market shifted.** A JNE agent in Denpasar, an agent since 2013, said volume fell from "hundreds of resi per day" to "at most 100, at least 50". His omzet fell "up to 60%". He named tighter competition as the cause: free pickup, free shipping, promos. He also said walk-ins were attracted when the counter accepted OVO payment, and that some cash payers switched to OVO "because of the discount".
- Source: Patroli Post interview, published 2019-11-02, interview held 2019-09-20. https://patrolipost.com/permudah-sistem-pembayaran-jne-gandeng-ovo/
- Tier: T3 (one owner, phone interview).
- Support: Direct, with partial quotes from the owner.
- Supports: J5 (customers do use non-cash payment at the counter), J13, J15. Counts as an anecdote.
- Stale: the account is from 2019.

**E2. Marketplace cashless payment reduced the agent to a commission earner.** A JNE agent in South Jakarta, an agent since 2009, said in his own words: "Pengirim tak lagi bayar langsung ke kami… tapi ke pusat… Kami dapat komisi saja." He dates this to about 2016.
- He also said JNE management required at least Rp10 juta omzet per month, and a new agent is reviewed over its first 3 months.
- He described a customer who was "menggebrak meja, bahkan nyaris memukul karyawan. Paketnya nyangkut."
- His tactics: opening until midnight, helping customers trace parcels with MyJNE and the call centre, and offering pickup above a minimum quantity.
- Source: MerahPutih feature, 2024-06-30. https://www.merahputih.com/post/read/agen-ekspedisi-bersiasat-cari-keuntungan-berlipat
- Tier: T3 (owner interview).
- Supports: J10 (the agent acts as the tracking help desk), J8 (pickup as a retention tool), J13.

**E3. Agents are closing in Yogyakarta.** Data attributed to JNE Cabang Utama Yogyakarta sales and marketing: **11 partners closed and 9 opened in 2025; 7 more closed by April 2026, with 1 opening**.
- Causes as narrated: aggregators "seperti KiriminAja dan Lincah.id" take walk-ins by offering pickup and COD outside marketplaces; shop rent and wages are rising; e-commerce is about 80% of volume but carries thinner margins than cash retail parcels; cashless commission payouts are often late.
- JNE's response: MyJNE and JNE Online Booking, aiming to cut counter transaction time to "di bawah 30 detik".
- Source: Gradasigo, 2026-05-19. https://www.gradasigo.com/article/siasat-jne-yogyakarta-jaga-ekosistem-logistik-di-tengah-tren-cashless
- Tier: T3. The figures are attributed to a courier branch, not a published dataset, and no owner is quoted.
- Supports: J1 (transaction speed matters), J13. Weighs **against** a walk-in-only thesis.

**E4. Drop-point commissions on marketplace parcels are tiny.** A Cilacap reader says marketplace-courier drop points earn "hanya Rp 200-an per paket", and "Rp 50 jika modenya pick up".
- He also says the hub takes over a seller's parcels once that seller grows, and that pickup-mode parcels a courier failed to collect cannot be scanned at the drop point, so the agent earns no commission on them.
- Source: Kompas.id "Redaksi Yth" letter, 2025-06-03. https://www.kompas.id/artikel/redaksi-yth-mitra-drop-point-ekspedisi-butuh-perhatian
- Tier: T3 (anecdote).
- Supports: J13. Inference: marketplace drop-off volume is low-margin, so a gerai's profit depends on retail (non-marketplace) senders paying the full tariff. That is exactly the segment GeraiExpress serves.

**E5. Advertised margins conflict across sources.**
- A Kompasiana practitioner guide says about **10–15%** per transaction. Its listed downsides: input mistakes cause financial loss, and slow service causes complaints and lost trust. Equipment it names as required: scale, tape measure, printer, deposit balance.
  - Source: Kompasiana, 2026-08-15. https://www.kompasiana.com/bagusprasetyo5376/6a8078c134777c0ade1eb6d3/homepreneur-part-4-agen-retail-ekspedisi
  - Tier: T3.
- Vendor blogs give about 20% (J&T agent) and from 25% (AutoKirim).
  - Sources: https://karebamedia.com/bisnis/rincian-penghasilan-agen-jnt (T3, SEO-leaning) and https://autokirim.com/agen (T2).
- Supports: J2 and J7 (input accuracy, deposit balance), J13. See Contradictions.

### B. Weight, volumetric weight and surcharges (J2)

**E6. Re-weighing after handover is a recurring, high-cost complaint.** Reader letters to Media Konsumen document couriers or sorting teams changing weight after handover, after which the seller's balance is deducted:
- 500 g billed as 6 kg. 2023-08-15. https://mediakonsumen.com/2023/08/15/surat-pembaca/baju-seberat-500-gram-ongkos-kirim-dihitung-6-kilogram
- 20 kg changed to 175 kg "oleh konter J&T Cargo", Rp465,000 deducted. 2024-06-02. https://mediakonsumen.com/2024/06/02/surat-pembaca/potongan-saldo-penjualan-shopee-gara-gara-jt-cargo-mengubah-berat-paket-dari-20-kg-menjadi-175-kg
- Recurrence complaint, where the claim was refused after 30 days. 2024-11-19. https://mediakonsumen.com/2024/11/19/surat-pembaca/terulang-kembali-saldo-seller-secara-sepihak-diambil-oleh-shopee-dengan-alasan-selisih-ongkir-jt-cargo
- 10 kg changed to 169 kg, Rp1.8 juta deducted. JNE, 2025-07-17. https://mediakonsumen.com/2025/07/17/surat-pembaca/potongan-rp18-juta-karena-selisih-ongkir-jne-dan-tokopedia-tiktok-shop-yang-tidak-masuk-akal
- Volumetric 24 kg changed to 77 kg. JNE, 2025-01-11. https://mediakonsumen.com/2025/01/11/surat-pembaca/potongan-saldo-penjual-shopee-gara-gara-jne-ekspedisi-mengubah-volume-dan-berat-barang-tidak-sesuai
- No option to cancel when the cashless fare changes after re-weighing. JNE, 2022-02-14. https://mediakonsumen.com/2022/02/14/surat-pembaca/tanpa-opsi-pembatalan-sistem-pengiriman-cashless-jne-merugikan-jika-ada-perubahan-ongkir-saat-berat-dihitung-ulang-tim-sortir

Tier: T3 (individual letters; the 2023 and 2024 bodies were 403 to direct fetch, so these are read from search excerpts). Support: Direct, at excerpt level. Supports J2 strongly.

Inference: in these cases the seller had no record of the measured weight and dimensions at handover to use as evidence. A gerai that records measured weight, dimensions and a photo at intake has an evidence pack.

**E7. An agent's own voice on volumetric disputes.** A Wahana agent in Bandar Lampung wrote that a 27×20×22 parcel was entered as 2 kg (formula P×L×T/6000) for Rp300,000. The Jakarta warehouse checker re-measured "secara lisan dengan penggaris dan tidak menghitung sampai ujung", which inflated the dimensions. The customer was charged more, and the agent was caught between the customer and the courier.
- Source: 2020-11-06. https://mediakonsumen.com/2020/11/06/surat-pembaca/wahana-prestasi-logistik-merugikan-agen-dan-konsumen
- Tier: T3 (anecdote; this is the only first-person **agent** account of this pain found).
- Supports: J2 (critical), J12. Stale: 2020.

**E8. Aggregators publish their own "selisih ongkir" rules.** Everpro's help centre explains that billing is based on actual or volumetric weight and lists causes of fare differences.
- Source: 2025-10-10. https://everpro.id/panduan/shipping/selisih-ongkir/
- Tier: T2.
- Interp.: competitors treat the weight difference as a routine support category. Supports J2.

### C. Lost and damaged parcels, claims (J12)

**E9. Lost-parcel letters show the same pattern: lost, slow investigation, compensation capped well below item value.**
- AnterAja lost parcel; compensation of 10× the shipping fee (Rp220,000), below the item's value. 2023-02-18. https://mediakonsumen.com/2023/02/18/surat-pembaca/paket-hilang-oleh-ekspedisi-anteraja-dan-dana-klaim-pengembalian-tidak-dibayar-seharga-barang
- 4 large JNE JTR parcels lost in one month, Rp4.4 juta loss. 2024-01-05. https://mediakonsumen.com/2024/01/05/surat-pembaca/dalam-sebulan-4-paket-besar-dinyatakan-hilang-kerugian-rp44-juta
- J&T: a 10 kg parcel posted at a J&T counter (Percetakan Negara) was declared lost 8 days later. 2024-09-08. https://mediakonsumen.com/2024/09/08/surat-pembaca/paket-besar-seberat-10-kg-dinyatakan-hilang-oleh-jt
- J&T lost parcel; refund still not processed months later. 2026-06-02. https://mediakonsumen.com/2026/06/02/surat-pembaca/paket-hilang-3-bulan-di-jt-solusi-tidak-efektif

Tier: T3. Supports J12 and J4: the 10× fee cap is the default when the parcel is not insured, which matches Permenkomdigi Art 73 in the gap analysis.

**E10. Academic survey of courier users.** 57% (77 respondents) reported a loss. 50.4% (54) had late delivery, 8.1% (12) damage and 4.4% (6) loss.
- Source: Jurnal (JBH) 14(1), 2025-07-10. https://doi.org/10.37893/jbh.v14i1.1027
- Tier: T3. The sample appears to be about 135. The search excerpt's percentages do not reconcile cleanly (77 of about 135 is 57%, but 54 is 40% of 135, not 50.4%). Sampling frame not read.
- Support: Direct, from the excerpt only.
- Supports: J10 and J12. Low confidence in the exact figures.

**E11. J&T's published rules: late-delivery money-back and an insurance ceiling.** The J&T help centre says late delivery beyond the SLA gives a "garansi uang kembali 100% sesuai ongkos kirim", claimed through the hotline with the resi. The J&T FAQ says insured claims are paid up to Rp50,000,000.
- Sources: https://help.jet.co.id/web-show/WFVsTnJ3eGFFUlpZbnkxQ3BWWG11UT09 and https://jet.co.id/information/faq
- Tier: T1/T2 (provider documents). Search excerpt only; direct fetch of the FAQ returned 418.
- Supports: J4, J12. Inference: a gerai that records the handover time (J8) can help a customer claim the SLA refund.

**E13. RTS rate on aggregators is "20–30%".** Everpro/Evermos say RTS "mencapai 20–30 persen pada banyak platform aggregator", against a global 16.9% (Statista 2023), and claim Everpro reduced its own RTS to an average of 8.2%.
- Sources: IDN Times, 2025-06-12 (fetched). https://www.idntimes.com/business/economy/begini-strategi-everpro-jne-kurangi-tingginya-angka-pengembalian-paket-00-brqf5-1fcscr and https://everpro.id/blog/shipping/evermos-jne-perkuat-sinergi/

- Support: Direct.

- Other figures: a 15–25% "rejection rate" from sellershipment.com and "2.5–3× prepaid" from codprem.com. Both are SEO content, T3, not used.

**E14. A seller quote on COD dependence.** A Surabaya skincare seller: "Sekarang 99 persen pembeli memilih COD. Revenue naik, tapi kendala retur juga tinggi."
- Source: Tribunnews, 2025-09-05. https://www.tribunnews.com/bisnis/7724513/memberi-rasa-aman-cod-masih-jadi-pilihan-favorit-konsumen-e-commerce
- Tier: T3 (anecdote). Supports J11.

**E15. Return parcels go missing at or near the agent.**
- A TikTok seller's COD return shows "Delivered to seller" but never arrived. She asked "setiap hari ke agen" (J&T Apartemen Laguna). 2024-04-28. https://mediakonsumen.com/2024/04/28/surat-pembaca/paket-retur-cod-saya-hilang-dan-tidak-ada-kejelasan
- J&T and Shopee blamed each other over a COD return the seller never received. 2021-09-05.
- Mengantar's own help page says AnterAja returns of manually dropped parcels go "only to that agent", and that SPX returns must be collected by the sender. Help page updated 2026-09-24 (fetched). https://help.mengantar.com/id/articles/5502313-jne-sicepat-sap-idexpress-jandt-ninja-xpress-lion-parcel-anteraja-atau-pos-ind

Tier: T3 for the letters, T1 for Mengantar. Supports J11. Interp.: a gerai can end up physically holding RTS parcels and answering for them, so RTS tracking is a counter job, not only a seller job.

**E16. Binjai SPX agent study.** "The Effect of COD on Return Rates: A Case Study on SPX Shopee Agents in Binjai", IJEBIR 5(3), April 2026, pp. 724–740.
- Abstract: COD "correlates with elevated goods return rates that significantly burden downstream logistical operations".
- The full text did not load (JavaScript page, and the DOI returned 404), so **no return-rate number was extracted**.

### E. Pickup versus drop-off, cut-offs, handover (J8)

**E17. Per-courier rules differ** (Mengantar help page, updated 2026-09-24, fetched):
- JNE: drop-off only at a **branch, not at agents**. Pickup cut-off 15:00.
- J&T: pickup, or drop-off at any J&T drop point. Cut-off 12:00.
- SiCepat: cut-off 12:00. iDexpress: cut-off 11:00. Pos: cut-off 16:00.
- AnterAja: pickup only.
- SPX: drop-off must be scanned immediately, or the sender must get a signed receipt showing the parcel count.
- Pickup SLA is 7–14 days.
- Tier: T1 (provider). Supports J8.
- Inference: the SPX rule is direct evidence that a handover receipt or manifest is a real operational need.

**E18. Couriers often miss pickups, and the drop point loses out.** Couriers fail to pick up because of "hujan dan overload", then tell sellers to drop the parcel at a drop point, where the agent cannot scan it (E4). A Reddit user (r/indonesiabebas, 2026-04-30) reports a J&T pickup that came 4 days late, after which the marketplace auto-cancelled the complaint.
- Tier: T3 (anecdotes). Supports J8.

**E19. Marketplaces push small sellers to drop-off.** Tokopedia: from 2023-10-01, request pickup for SiCepat and "Kurir Rekomendasi" requires at least **125 orders in the last 30 days**. Below that, sellers drop off.
- Source: https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/pengiriman-drop-off-dan-pick-up (T2; search excerpt only, the fetch timed out).
- Interp.: this creates drop-off foot traffic. It is marketplace drop-off, which earns the low commission described in E4.

### F. Customer communication and tracking (J9, J10)

**E20. Agents act as the tracking help desk.** The South Jakarta JNE owner (E2) says the agency helps "telusuri" parcels that are late or lost, using MyJNE and the call centre. A JNE Yogyakarta agent (E3) "proactively follows up on shipment status" as a retention tactic.
- Tier: T3. Supports J10.
- Missing evidence: how many such enquiries per day, and through which channel. Not found anywhere.

**E21. Small businesses sell and talk to customers mainly through messaging apps.**
- **BPS 2024:** about 4.4 million e-commerce business units. Snippet: 94.76% sell through instant messaging. Databoks, 2025-12-03 (paywalled; lead sentence fetched, the exact share is from the search snippet). https://databoks.katadata.co.id/teknologi-telekomunikasi/statistik/692fe011736e4/aplikasi-pesan-instan-saluran-utama-usaha-e-commerce-di-indonesia — T1 via T2.
- **BPS 2021:** 93.98% sell through instant messaging. Stale.
- **Mandiri Business Survey 2024:** 523 small business owners; 60% sold online; 69% of those through instant messaging. T2, Databoks excerpt.
- **DAI/Ipsos for Facebook:** face-to-face CAPI, n≈1,043, June 2021, 11 provinces, not nationally representative. 54–55% of online MSMEs used WhatsApp to talk to customers in the past 30 days. https://www.dai.com/uploads/final-msme-reports/indonesia-country-brief.pdf (fetched) — T2, commissioned by Meta. Stale.
- Supports J9 strongly (WhatsApp is the default channel). Inference: senders who are online sellers will want a resi message they can forward over WhatsApp.

**E22. Tracking matters to people choosing a courier.**
- Populix, May 2023, 1,557 Muslim respondents aged 17–45 (Gen Z cut): "a physical tracking number" mattered to 62%, and efficient time and cost to 71%. Kompas Tekno, 2023-08-06 (fetched). https://tekno.kompas.com/copy/2023/08/06/09000027/5-jasa-ekspedisi-favorit-gen-z-di-indonesia-untuk-belanja-online — T2.
- Snapcart, n=500 Shopee buyers, April–June 2025, online survey: shipping price 74%, speed 64%, on-time delivery 60%. Kompas Money, 2025-07-18. https://money.kompas.com/read/2025/07/18/214624726/survei-kecepatan-dan-ketepatan-pengiriman-jadi-faktor-kunci-e-commerce — T2.
- Supports J1 (price comparison) and J9/J10.

### G. Courier preference among senders (J1)

**E23. Gen Z uses J&T most, for both online purchases and personal shipping.** Populix (as E22):

| Courier | Online purchases | Personal shipping |
|---|---|---|
| J&T | 58% | 55% |
| JNE | 27% | 34% |
| SPX | 32% | 18% |
| SiCepat | 23% | 17% |
| GoSend | 7% | 16% |
| Pos | 3% | 7% |

- Tier: T2. Interp.: personal (non-marketplace) senders lean more to JNE, GoSend, Pos and TIKI than online shoppers do, so a counter needs JNE plus J&T at minimum.
- Gap: the sample is Muslim respondents aged 17–45, and the Gen Z subset size was not stated. Supports J1.

### H. Payment at the counter: cash, QRIS, e-wallet (J5, J6)

**E24. QRIS is nearly universal among small merchants.**
- BI, via CNBC Indonesia, 2025-09-17 (fetched): **40 million QRIS merchants and 57.6 million users by August 2025**, with 93% of merchants UMKM. T1 via T2.
- ASPI, via Databoks: 39 million merchants in Q2 2025, up 18% year on year. https://databoks.katadata.co.id/keuangan/statistik/68e8a2e1b3b22/jumlah-merchant-qris-tumbuh-18-pada-kuartal-ii-2025
- Supports J5. Inference: a walk-in sender expects to be able to pay by QRIS, so the counter needs to record "cash vs QRIS vs transfer" per shipment and reconcile it.

**E25. Counter payment is not well documented by the couriers.** A search-tool synthesis said the J&T FAQ tells non-corporate drop-point customers to pay in cash. I **could not verify this**: the FAQ returned HTTP 418, and `source_check` found no passage. **Not used as evidence.**
- E1 (2019) is the only first-hand source on non-cash payment at a counter, and it is an anecdote.
- Fastpay and SIMASRIM market QRIS soundboxes and EDCs to agents (gap analysis N5). T2.

**E26. Manual bookkeeping is a documented problem at small shipping agents.** An academic community-service paper on BJ-X Agen Purwoharjo (4 staff) says the owner had problems recording each receipt and expense. A Surabaya "Galeri Ekspedisi" internship report notes manual recording in Google Drive.
- Sources: https://doi.org/10.38048/jailcb.v4i4.2250 (2023-11-30; abstract excerpt only, fetch 406) and https://repository.dinamika.ac.id/id/eprint/7505/ — T3.
- Supports J6 and J13. This is the only evidence found for a day-close or bookkeeping pain, and it is weak.

## 3. Top 10 operator pains, ranked by evidence strength

Strength combines the number of independent sources, their tier, recency, and whether the source speaks for the **counter** rather than only the seller.

1. **Falling walk-in volume and thin, delayed margins**, from marketplace cashless payment, free pickup, aggregators and marketplace drop-point commissions of about Rp200 per parcel (J13). Evidence: E1, E2, E3, E4, E5. Several owner voices plus branch data.
2. **Weight and volumetric disputes after handover** (J2). Evidence: E6 (6+ letters, 2020–2025), E7 (agent), E8.
3. **COD returns (RTS) and lost return parcels** (J11). Evidence: E12 (4 surveys), E13 (20–30% vendor figure), E14, E15, E16.
4. **Lost or damaged parcels and slow, capped claims** (J12, J4). Evidence: E9 (4+ letters), E10, E11.

## 4. Evidence gaps only a pilot or interviews can close

- **Counter payment mix:** cash, QRIS, transfer, e-wallet, per shipment. No survey measures this at shipping counters.
- **Parcels per day per gerai, and the split between walk-in retail, online-seller drop-off and marketplace drop-off.** Only anecdotes exist (E1: 50–100 per day in 2019; E2: 1–10 per day at a failed site).
- **Share of shipments that lead to a weight dispute, an RTS, or a claim**, and who absorbs the cost: the gerai, the sender, or the courier.
- **How many "where is my parcel" enquiries a gerai gets per day, through which channel, and how long each takes.**
- **Cash variance at day close**, and whether owners reconcile at all. No evidence found.
- **Willingness to pay** for software versus taking a lower commission, and whether owners would switch from bosAgen, Fastpay or AutoKirim.
- **Whether walk-in senders want a printed receipt, a WhatsApp receipt, or neither.**
- **Whether a gerai is allowed to accept COD for non-marketplace senders** (the "COD tanpa marketplace" that aggregators advertise), and whether owners want it.
- **The actual RTS rate for counter-originated COD** on Mengantar (E13 is aggregator-wide and vendor-stated).

## 5. Proposed owner interview guide (Bahasa Indonesia, 10 questions, about 30 minutes)

Interviewer notes: ask for the **last concrete example** rather than an opinion ("Ceritakan kejadian terakhir…"). Record numbers as ranges if the owner is unsure. Do not show GeraiExpress before question 9.

1. **Volume dan jenis pengirim.** "Dalam seminggu terakhir, kira-kira berapa paket per hari yang Bapak/Ibu kirim? Dari jumlah itu, berapa yang pelanggan datang langsung (umum), berapa dari penjual online yang setor paket, dan berapa paket marketplace yang tinggal di-drop?"
2. **Pembayaran di kasir.** "Pelanggan biasanya bayar pakai apa: tunai, QRIS, transfer, atau dompet digital? Kira-kira berapa persen masing-masing? Apakah pelanggan minta nota atau bukti bayar, dan dalam bentuk apa (kertas, foto, WhatsApp)?"
3. **Tutup kas harian.** "Bagaimana cara Bapak/Ibu mencocokkan uang di laci dengan resi yang keluar hari itu? Kapan terakhir ada selisih, berapa nilainya, dan apa penyebabnya?"
4. **Timbangan dan selisih ongkir.** "Ceritakan kejadian terakhir ketika berat atau ukuran paket diubah oleh ekspedisi setelah paket diserahkan. Siapa yang menanggung selisihnya, dan bukti apa yang Bapak/Ibu punya?"
5. **Pertanyaan 'paket saya di mana?'.** "Dalam sehari, berapa kali pelanggan menanyakan posisi paket? Lewat apa (datang langsung, telepon, WhatsApp)? Berapa lama rata-rata untuk menjawabnya?"
6. **Retur dan COD.** "Apakah Bapak/Ibu melayani kiriman COD? Dari 10 paket COD, kira-kira berapa yang kembali (retur)? Siapa yang menanggung ongkir retur, dan di mana paket retur itu disimpan atau diambil?"
7. **Paket hilang atau rusak dan klaim.** "Ceritakan klaim paket hilang atau rusak yang terakhir. Berapa lama prosesnya, berapa yang diganti, dan apa peran Bapak/Ibu dalam mengurusnya? Apakah pelanggan ditawari asuransi waktu kirim?"
8. **Pickup dan serah terima ke kurir.** "Kurir lebih sering menjemput atau Bapak/Ibu yang mengantar ke cabang? Seberapa sering kurir telat atau tidak datang? Apakah ada bukti serah terima (tanda tangan, scan, daftar paket)?"
9. **Alat yang dipakai sekarang.** "Aplikasi atau sistem apa saja yang dipakai sekarang (aplikasi ekspedisi, agregator seperti bosAgen/Fastpay/AutoKirim, buku, Excel)? Apa yang paling menyebalkan dari alat itu? Kalau besok alat itu hilang, apa yang paling Bapak/Ibu rindukan?"
10. **Penghasilan dan kesediaan bayar.** "Kira-kira berapa persen keuntungan per resi yang Bapak/Ibu terima sekarang, dan kapan dibayarkan? Kalau ada aplikasi yang menyelesaikan masalah nomor [sebutkan masalah terbesar dari jawaban di atas], Bapak/Ibu lebih memilih bayar langganan bulanan, potongan per resi, atau tidak mau bayar sama sekali? Berapa batas wajarnya?"

## Contradictions

- **Counter margin.** The Kompasiana guide says 10–15% per transaction (E5). Vendor and SEO pages say about 20% (J&T) and from 25% (AutoKirim). A Kompas.id letter says about Rp200 per parcel for marketplace drop points (E4). These are different products (retail agency, aggregator, marketplace drop point), not one figure.
- **Jakpat COD 64%.** GoodStats attributes it to a Jakpat survey of 1,931 respondents in May 2025. A search-tool synthesis attributes 64% to a Jakpat H2-2024 report. Unresolved: GoodStats returned HTTP 520.
- **COD share** ranges from 56% (NielsenIQ 2023, 5 cities) to 68.2% (APJII 2026). The methods differ: "main method" versus "used". The two cannot be directly compared.
- **RTS rate.** 20–30% (Everpro, "many aggregators") versus 8.2% (Everpro's own claim) versus 15–25% (SEO site, rejected). All are vendor-stated.
- **Walk-in channel outlook.** E3 (agents closing, aggregators taking walk-ins) cuts against a walk-in counter thesis. Aggregator vendors (gap analysis §3.2) claim thousands of active agents. Both can be true: the agent network churns while multi-courier agents grow.
